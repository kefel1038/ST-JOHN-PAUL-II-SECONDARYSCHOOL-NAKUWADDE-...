About Us

Our Story: A Creative Space for a Mind

St. John Paul II Day & Boarding Secondary School Nakuwadde - Bulenga was founded in 2016 with a vision to provide quality, accessible secondary education rooted in Catholic values. Located in Nakuwadde, near Bulenga Trading Centre along the Kampala-Mityana Road in Wakiso District, our school has grown from humble beginnings into a vibrant community of 800+ learners.

Named after St. John Paul II, a pope known for his commitment to youth, education, and interfaith dialogue, our institution embodies his legacy by nurturing learners into responsible, skilled, and morally upright citizens who can transform their communities.

Our Mission

To provide holistic, quality education that integrates academic excellence, vocational skills, and spiritual formation, producing competent, innovative, and God-fearing citizens.

Our Vision

To be a leading center of academic excellence, innovation, and character formation in Uganda and beyond.

2016
School founded with a small cohort of students, focusing on O-Level education.
2019
First UCE cohort sat examinations with impressive results. A-Level program introduced.
2021
Vocational skills program launched. ICT laboratory established.
2024
School enrollment crossed 800 students. Boarding facilities expanded. New science lab built.
2026
Ongoing campus development: new classrooms, vocational workshops, and sports grounds.
