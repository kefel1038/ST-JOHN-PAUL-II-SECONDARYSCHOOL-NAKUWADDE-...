About Us

Building Tomorrow's Leaders Today

St. John Paul II Day & Boarding Secondary School Nakuwadde - Bulenga is a community-based mixed day and boarding secondary school dedicated to nurturing learners into responsible, skilled, and morally upright citizens.

Founded on Catholic values and open to all faiths, we provide quality education combining academic excellence, vocational training, and character formation.

Why Choose Us

What Makes Us Different

We offer a unique blend of academic rigor, vocational training, and values-based education that sets our learners up for lifelong success.

Competency-Based Curriculum

Modern CBC framework focused on critical thinking, creativity, communication, and real-world problem-solving skills.

Vocational & Practical Skills

Agriculture, ICT, tailoring, carpentry, entrepreneurship — students graduate with both academic qualifications and marketable skills.

Character & Faith Formation

Grounded in Catholic values yet open to all, we nurture integrity, compassion, and spiritual growth in every student.

Qualified & Dedicated Staff

Our team of 45+ qualified teachers provides individual attention and mentorship to help every student succeed.

Sports & Co-Curricular

Football, netball, athletics, debate, drama, music — a vibrant co-curricular program that develops well-rounded students.

Safe & Supportive Environment

Separate boys and girls dormitories, 24/7 supervision, nutritious meals, and a caring community that feels like family.
