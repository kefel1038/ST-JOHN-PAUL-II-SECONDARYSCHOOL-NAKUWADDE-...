About Our School

Discover our story, our vision, and the dedicated community behind St. John Paul II Day & Boarding Secondary School Nakuwadde - Bulenga.

Who We Are

Building Tomorrow's Leaders Today

St. John Paul II Day & Boarding Secondary School Nakuwadde - Bulenga is a community-based mixed day and boarding secondary school dedicated to nurturing learners into responsible, skilled, and morally upright citizens of Uganda and the world.

Founded on Catholic values and open to learners of all faiths, the school provides quality education that combines academic excellence, vocational training, leadership development, and character formation. We are proudly located at Nakuwadde, near Bulenga Trading Centre on the Kampala-Mityana Road, Wakiso District.

Named in honour of Pope St. John Paul II - a global champion of youth, education, and human dignity - our school carries forward his legacy of faith-driven excellence. We believe that every child, regardless of background, deserves access to quality education that shapes their character and unlocks their God-given potential.

Our Vision
To be a centre of academic excellence, character formation and vocational training that produces responsible and innovative citizens.
Our Mission
To provide holistic, affordable, and quality education that nurtures academic excellence, practical skills, moral values, and spiritual growth.

Our History

Our Journey of Growth

From humble beginnings to a thriving educational community - the story of St. John Paul II Day & Boarding SS Nakuwadde - Bulenga.

2016
Foundation
St. John Paul II Day & Boarding Secondary School Nakuwadde - Bulenga was established by the local Catholic community of Nakuwadde to provide quality secondary education to children in the area along the Kampala-Mityana Road.
2017
First Students Enrolled
The school welcomed its pioneer class of students in Senior One, beginning the journey of academic excellence that continues to grow each year.
2019
Government Recognition
The school received full recognition and accreditation from the Uganda National Examinations Board (UNEB) and Ministry of Education, enabling students to sit national examinations.
2020
Vocational Programs Launched
The school launched its unique vocational education department offering Agriculture, ICT, Tailoring, and Entrepreneurship, setting it apart from other schools in the region.
2022
First UCE Graduates
Our pioneer Senior Four class sat the Uganda Certificate of Education exams with impressive results, many proceeding to upper secondary and tertiary institutions.
2024-Present
Expansion & Excellence
New computer lab, expanded dormitory facilities, and additional vocational workshops. The school continues to grow in enrollment, programs, and academic achievement.
