CBC Approach

Competency-Based Curriculum

Uganda's modern CBC framework shifts focus from rote memorisation to developing real-world skills and competencies.

01

Critical Thinking

Students learn to analyse, evaluate, and synthesise information to make informed decisions and solve complex real-world problems.

02

Creativity & Innovation

Through project-based learning, design thinking, and entrepreneurship, students develop creative approaches to challenges.

03

Communication

English literacy, debate, presentations, and written communication skills essential for leadership and professional life.

04

Collaboration

Group work, team projects, and community service build the interpersonal and teamwork skills employers and universities value.

05

Digital Citizenship

Responsible, ethical, and skilled use of technology in academic work, communication, and entrepreneurship.

06

Self-Management

Discipline, time management, resilience, and goal-setting skills that students carry with them through life.
