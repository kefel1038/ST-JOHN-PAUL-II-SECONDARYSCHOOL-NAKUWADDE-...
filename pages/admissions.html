How to Apply

Simple 4-Step Process

Joining St. John Paul II Day & Boarding Secondary School Nakuwadde - Bulenga is straightforward. Follow these four steps to secure your child's place.

1
Fill the Inquiry / Application Form
Complete the online inquiry form on this page or visit our admissions office in person. Forms are also available at the school gate.
2
Submit Required Documents
Bring original and photocopy of Primary Leaving Certificate (PLE) or previous school reports, birth certificate, and 4 passport-size photos.
3
Visit the Campus
We encourage all prospective students and parents to visit the school for a guided tour and to meet our teaching staff and administration.
4
Confirm Admission & Pay Fees
Upon acceptance, you will receive an admission letter. Confirm your place by paying the first term school fees and completing registration.

Boarding Life

A Home Away from Home

Our boarding facility provides a safe, structured, and supportive home environment where students can focus on their studies, build lasting friendships, and develop independence.

Supervised Study Time: Evening prep every night with teacher supervision to complete homework and review lessons.
Nutritious Meals: Three balanced meals daily — breakfast, lunch, and supper — planned for student health and energy.
24/7 Supervision: Trained house parents and matrons on duty at all times for safety and welfare.
Separate Dormitories: Boys and girls dormitories are completely separate with full security.
Spiritual Formation: Morning devotions, weekly chapel, and pastoral care for all boarding students.
Medical Care: First aid provisions and access to nearby medical facilities for emergency care.
FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about admissions, fees, and school life.

What are the academic requirements for S1 admission?
Students applying for Senior One are required to have sat and passed the Primary Leaving Examination (PLE). We consider aggregates from 4 to 36. Students with special learning needs are assessed on a case-by-case basis. We believe every child deserves a chance at quality education.
Is the school open to non-Catholic students?
Absolutely. While St. John Paul II Day & Boarding Secondary School Nakuwadde - Bulenga is founded on Catholic values, we warmly welcome students of all religious backgrounds — Christian, Muslim, and others. We celebrate diversity and nurture universal values of integrity, service, and respect for all faiths.
Can students transfer from other schools mid-year?
Yes, we accept transfer students throughout the year subject to space availability. You will need a transfer letter from your previous school, copies of your most recent school reports, and all standard admission documents. Please contact our admissions office to check class availability.
What is the school uniform?
All students wear the official school uniform: a white short-sleeve shirt/blouse with the school crest, royal blue trousers (boys) or royal blue skirt (girls), blue tie, black shoes, and white socks. The full uniform set can be purchased from the school uniform shop on campus.
Are there scholarships or bursaries available?
We are committed to making quality education accessible. Deserving students from low-income families may be considered for partial fee support. Please speak confidentially with our director or admissions officer about your specific situation. We evaluate each case with compassion and fairness.
What vocational programs are available?
We offer 8 vocational programs: Agriculture, Poultry Farming, Computer Skills, Entrepreneurship, Tailoring & Fashion, Carpentry, Art & Handcrafts, and Financial Literacy. These run alongside the academic curriculum and are available to all students across all classes.
How do I pay school fees?
Fees can be paid directly at the school bursar's office in cash, or via bank deposit. Mobile money payments may be arranged through our administration. Please contact us on +256 703 262 686 for current payment details and bank account information.
