Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Introduction

St. John Paul II Day & Boarding Secondary School Nakuwadde - Bulenga respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal data you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or interact with us.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Identification Information: Name, phone number, email address, and any details provided through our contact or admissions forms.
  • Academic Information: Previous school records, class applying for, and related academic details provided during the admissions process.
  • Usage Data: Non-personal information such as browser type, pages visited, time spent on the site, and referring URLs.

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data for the following purposes:

  • To process admissions inquiries and applications
  • To respond to your questions, comments, or complaints
  • To send school news, events, and academic updates (with your consent)
  • To improve our website and services
  • To comply with legal obligations

Data Protection

We implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

Third-Party Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or transfer your personal information to third parties without your consent, except as required by law or for providing services you have requested (e.g., Google Maps for location services).

Cookies

Our website may use cookies to enhance your browsing experience. You can choose to disable cookies in your browser settings, though this may affect some website functionality.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent for data processing
